Traefik supports WS and WSS out of the box. Guide covers TLS, entrypoints, Headers middleware for Origin, insecureSkipVerify for self-signed backends.

No WAF middleware, no skip-on-Upgrade, no statement that Upgrade headers bypass inspection.
